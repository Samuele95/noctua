What it is

The skill’s own description, unedited. It stays in English because it is the package’s text, not ours.

Geometric, qualitative, neurosymbolic analysis of a tabular dataset: its effective and intrinsic DIMENSIONS, its BASIS (the minimal set of mutually non-derivable features, every other column expressed as a derivation rule), the residual DEPENDENCIES among basis members, and the FEATURE/LABEL partitions they imply, modeled as a domain-forge ontology (T-box, A-box sample, SWRL/Horn rules) so the engines verify what the model proposes, plus compact statistics. Emits a domain-forge-compatible .domain.html with an interactive geometry explorer, input to /model-chat, /inferred-questions, /data-lens and a /domain-forge refine. Trigger when the user hands over a dataset and asks about its dimensions, span, basis, redundant or derived columns, feature/label separation, a dataset ontology or knowledge base, a geometric or neurosymbolic data analysis, or invokes /dataset-forge. Sibling forge of /domain-forge. Not for profiling or statistics (/data-lens), transforming data (/dataset-shaper), or prose (/domain-forge).
